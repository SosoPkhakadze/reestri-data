# reestri-data

Open, reproducible analyses built on the Reestri indexes of public registers and procurement records in Georgia, Armenia and Moldova. Aggregate only; no individuals are named.

- [am-contracts](am-contracts/): Armenian public procurement, 2018 to 2026, from the official PPCM registry. Three analyses with 14 CSV tables, methods and scripts: single-source awards, where the non-competitive money moved, and the test of the end-of-year spending rush (there is none; the money is signed in January) alongside the rise of urgent procedures.

Source tools: https://apify.com/reestri
