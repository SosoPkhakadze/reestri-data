# Draft post (r/datasets or LinkedIn), about 250 words

**We went looking for Armenia's December procurement rush. It is not there. The money is signed in January.**

Third pass over the PPCM index, Armenia's public contracts registry: 235,570 signed contracts, snapshot of
6 September 2026. The premise was the familiar one, budget money spent in a hurry before 31 December.
Across the eight complete years 2018 to 2025 (192,369 contracts, 3,958,215,584,070 AMD) the registry says
otherwise.

December holds 20,094 contracts, 10.45 percent of the count, against 8.33 percent for an even month. But
its 424,043,419,447 AMD is 10.71 percent of the value. January holds 638,976,946,239 AMD, 16.14 percent,
1.94 times an even month and 1.51 times December's money. December's value share falls below an even month
in four of the eight years: 3.46 percent in 2018, 6.74 in 2022, 7.42 in 2023, 6.10 in 2025.

At buyer level there is no cliff either. Of 129 buyers with at least 50 contracts and 500 million AMD over
those years, the median share of value signed in December is 7.69 percent, below an even month. One is
above 50 percent. December contracts are not bigger (median 450,000 AMD against 434,850 for the rest of
the year) and are barely more urgent (29.79 percent of value against 29.43).

What January is: the state medical order. In January 2018 the Ministry of Health signed 73.19 percent of
the month's money, and 89.24 percent of the month was awarded single source.

The real shift is elsewhere. Urgent procedures now carry 3.79 percent of contracts and 27.59 percent of all
value, up from 10.70 percent of value in 2018 to 42.07 percent in 2024. Two buyers hold 61.40 percent of
it: the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Infrastructure (418,145,449,528 AMD, 58.02 percent of
its own spend) and the Armenian Territorial Development Fund (347,643,256,065 AMD, 64.75 percent).

Method, every exclusion and five CSVs:
https://github.com/SosoPkhakadze/reestri-data/tree/main/am-contracts
Aggregate only, state buyers and companies only.

The index behind it is queryable per supplier by tax ID or name, refreshed weekly:
https://apify.com/reestri/am-supplier-contracts
