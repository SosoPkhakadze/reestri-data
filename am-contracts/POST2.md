# Draft post (r/datasets or LinkedIn), about 250 words

**Armenia's single-source public money left the Ministry of Health. It went to the public broadcaster, a probation service and a tech ministry.**

Second pass over the 236,159 signed contracts in PPCM, Armenia's procurement portal, by buyer, supplier and contract size, 2018 to August 2026.

In 2018 the Ministry of Health awarded 62.9 billion AMD without competition, 95.70 percent of its spend. In 2026 so far: 0.8 billion, 5.98 percent. The largest single-source buyer of 2024 to 2026 is the Council of Public Broadcaster (22.9 billion AMD, 93.27 percent of its 2026 spend), then Yerevan Municipality, the Ministry of High-Tech Industry (2.93 percent single-source in 2024, 68.16 percent in 2026) and the Probation Service (99.64 percent in 2026). 34 of 185 active buyers still award more than half their spend single-source.

What else moved:

- The supplier base grew from 3,072 winners in 2018 to 7,795 in 2024. The top 10 suppliers' share of value fell from 30.12 to 17.24 percent.
- More than a third of each year's winners are new to the registry: 2,573 first-time suppliers in 2022, 2,917 in 2024. The 15 largest newcomers since 2022 won 358.0 billion AMD; 12 of them have a single-source share of exactly zero.
- The median contract shrank from 569,600 AMD to 330,000 AMD in 2025, while contracts above 1 billion AMD went from 21 (17.78 percent of value) to 243 in 2024 (59.48 percent).

Method, caveats (first seen in PPCM is not first ever; 2026 is year to date) and four CSVs: https://github.com/SosoPkhakadze/reestri-data/tree/main/am-contracts Aggregate only, companies only.

The index behind it is queryable per supplier by tax ID or name, refreshed weekly: https://apify.com/reestri/am-supplier-contracts
